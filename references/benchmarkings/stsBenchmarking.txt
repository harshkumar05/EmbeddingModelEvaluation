The Source Paper: "Sentence-BERT: Sentence Embeddings using Siamese BERT-Networks" (Reimers & Gurevych, 2019)

This paper is the "Bible" for sentence embeddings. The researchers proved that standard LLMs (like raw BERT) are actually terrible at comparing sentences until they are specially fine-tuned.

(Note: STS scores are generally higher than SimLex scores because full sentences provide more clues for the math to grab onto).
